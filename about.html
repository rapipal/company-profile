About Google

Technology should be useful for everyone.

We build products and services that make information more accessible and help people live, work, and learn better.

Our Story

From a university project to a global technology company.

  1. 1998
    1998

    Founded in a Garage

    Larry Page and Sergey Brin founded Google in a Menlo Park garage, with a mission to organize the world's information.

  2. 2004
    2004

    Gmail & IPO

    Google launched Gmail with 1GB of storage — revolutionary at the time — and went public on the Nasdaq.

  3. 2008
    2008

    Android & Chrome

    The first Android-powered smartphone shipped, and Google launched Chrome — beginning a new chapter in mobile and web.

  4. 2015
    2015

    Alphabet Formed

    Google restructured under Alphabet Inc., separating core products from longer-term research and investment initiatives.

  5. 2019
    2019

    Quantum Milestone

    Google's quantum computer achieved a calculation in 200 seconds that would take the fastest classical computer 10,000 years.

  6. 2023
    2023

    AI at the Center

    Gemini launched as Google's most capable AI model, integrating intelligence across Search, Workspace, and Cloud.

Our Mission
"Organize the world's information and make it universally accessible and useful."

This mission has guided every product decision and research investment since 1998. It shapes how we build Search, how we approach AI, and how we think about our responsibility to users and society.
